Users may recover a forgotten password from an application sign-in page by following the steps below.

:::note[Before you begin]
Administrators should <Edition only="cloud">[enable password recovery](../../flows/password-recovery.mdx)</Edition><Edition only="self-hosted">enable password recovery</Edition> for users of the organization.
:::

## Recover a forgotten password

:::warning
Users that onboard from a read-only remote user store are not able to recover their passwords through the My Account portal.
:::

To reset the password, the user should follow these steps.

1. Go to a login page via a business application or the [My Account portal](./configure-self-service-portal.mdx).

    <Variants><Edition only="cloud"><img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/recover-your-password.png").default} alt="Recover your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition><Edition only="self-hosted"><img src={require("../../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/recover-your-password.png").default} alt="Recover your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition></Variants>

2. Click **Forgot password?**

3. Follow one of the following methods based on the password recovery option configured for your organization.

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-link" label="Email link">

    Enter your username and click **Send Reset Link**. An email notification will be sent to your email address.
    <Variants><Edition only="cloud"><img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-email-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition><Edition only="self-hosted"><img src={require("../../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-email-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition></Variants>

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    <Edition only="cloud">

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-otp" label="Email OTP">

    Enter your username and click **Send Email OTP**. An OTP code will be sent to your registered email address.
    <img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-email-otp.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    </Edition>

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="sms-otp" label="SMS OTP">

    Enter your username and click **Send SMS OTP**. An OTP code will be sent to your registered mobile number.
    <Variants><Edition only="cloud"><img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-sms-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition><Edition only="self-hosted"><img src={require("../../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-sms-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition></Variants>

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    :::note
    - Setting your email address and mobile number is mandatory to use the email link and SMS OTP recovery methods respectively.

    - If the organization has configured multiple options for password recovery, select your preferred method and click **Proceed**.
    :::

4. Confirm password reset request.

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-link" label="Email link">

    Open the email you received and click **Reset Password**.
    <img src={require("../../../../assets/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-email.png").default} alt="Reset password email" width="500" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    <Edition only="cloud">

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-otp" label="Email OTP">

    Enter the OTP code sent to your email and click **Continue**.
    <img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-email-otp.png").default} alt="Reset password email OTP" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    </Edition>

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="sms-otp" label="SMS OTP">

    Enter the OTP code sent to your mobile and click **Continue**.
    <Variants><Edition only="cloud"><img src={require("../../../../assets/cloud/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-sms-otp.png").default} alt="Reset password SMS OTP" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition><Edition only="self-hosted"><img src={require("../../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-sms-otp.png").default} alt="Reset password SMS OTP" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition></Variants>

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

5. Enter new password and click **Proceed**.

:::note
If the user is already signed in to the My Account portal, the password can be reset from there. Learn how to [change password from the My Account portal](./change-password.mdx).
:::

## Check password recovery email
Users can also check the email address and mobile number to which their password recovery information is sent.

1. In the My Account portal, navigate to **Security** > **Account Recovery**.

    <Variants><Edition only="cloud"><img src={require("../../../../assets/img/guides/users/account-recovery.png").default} alt="Account recovery in WSO2 Identity Platform MyAccount" width="700" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition><Edition only="self-hosted"><img src={require("../../../../assets/img/guides/users/account-recovery.png").default} alt="Account recovery in WSO2 Identity Server MyAccount" width="700" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} /></Edition></Variants>

2. Click the eye icon of the relevant row, to check the recovery email address or the recovery mobile.

3. Click **Done**.
