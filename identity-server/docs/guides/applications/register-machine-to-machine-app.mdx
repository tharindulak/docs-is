To integrate your machine-to-machine (M2M) application with {edition.productName}, you need to first register your application from the {edition.productName} Console. 
Then you can authorize your M2M applications to access management APIs of {edition.productName} or your business APIs.
A client ID and client secret are issued to the application upon registration.

Follow the instructions given below.

## Register the app

To register the app:

1. On the {edition.productName} Console, click **Applications**.

    <img src={require("../../../assets/img/guides/applications/select-app-type.png").default} alt="Select app type" width="700" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

2. Click **New Application** and select **M2M Application**.

3. In the **Name** field, enter a unique name to identify your application.

4. Click **Register** to complete the registration.

5. [Enable the application](./index.mdx#enabledisable-an-application) when it is ready for use.

## Get the client ID and secret

When you register your M2M application, a client ID and client secret are generated. Your M2M application will identify itself to {edition.productName} with these credentials.

You can get this client ID and client secret from the **Protocol** tab of the application, as shown below.
