If you are the owner or an administrator of an {edition.productName} organization, the following guide explains how you can recover a forgotten password.

To recover your password:

1. Go to the sign-in page of the [My Account Portal](./is-self-service.mdx#access-my-account-portal).

    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/recover-your-password.png").default} alt="Recover your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

2. Click **Forgot password?**.

3. Follow one of the following methods that are configured for your organization.

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-link" label="Email link">

    Enter your username and click **Send Reset Link**. An email notification will be sent to your email address.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-email-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    <TabItem value="email-otp" label="Email OTP">

    Enter your username and click **Send Email OTP**. An OTP code will be sent to your registered email address.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-email-otp.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    <TabItem value="sms-otp" label="SMS OTP">

    Enter your username and click **Send SMS OTP**. An OTP code will be sent to your registered mobile number.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/password-recovery-option-sms-only.png").default} alt="Forgot your password" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

    :::note
    - Setting an email address is required for email link and email OTP recovery methods, while setting a mobile number is required for SMS OTP recovery.

    - If you have configured both options for password recovery, select your preferred method and click **Proceed**.
    :::

4. Confirm password reset request.

    <Tabs groupId="tabs">
    <TabItem value="email-link" label="Email link">

    Open the email you received and click **Reset Password**.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-email.png").default} alt="Reset password email" width="500" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    <TabItem value="email-otp" label="Email OTP">

    Enter the OTP code sent to your email and click **Continue**.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-email-otp.png").default} alt="Reset password email OTP" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    <TabItem value="sms-otp" label="SMS OTP">

    Enter the OTP code sent to your mobile and click **Continue**.
    <img src={require("../../../assets/self-hosted/img/guides/organization/self-service/customer/reset-password-sms-otp.png").default} alt="Reset password SMS OTP" width="300" style={{display: "block", margin: "0", border: "0.3px solid lightgrey"}} />

    </TabItem>
    </Tabs>

5. Enter new password and **Proceed**.
