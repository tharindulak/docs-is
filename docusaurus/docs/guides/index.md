# Guides - Overview

The guides in this section provide the instructions for building identity and access management uses cases and managing them.

## Developer guide

If you are an application developer, you can integrate your applications with WSO2 Identity Platform to implement identity and access management use cases for your application.

See the following sections for details:

- [Applications](applications/index.md)
- [Authentication](authentication/index.md)
- [API Authorization](authorization/api-authorization/api-authorization.md)
- [Identity Verification](identity-verification/index.md)
- [Branding](branding/index.md)


## Administration guide

The administration guides cover the topics related to managing the users in your organizations and other organization settings.

- [User Management](users/index.md)
- [Account Configurations](user-accounts/index.md)
- [User Self-Service](user-self-service/index.md)
- [Organizations](organization-management/index.md)

## Your WSO2 Identity Platform guide

Manage your WSO2 Identity Platform organization and your administration user account. See the topics in the [Your WSO2 Identity Platform](your-identity-platform/index.md) section for details.

</Applies>
