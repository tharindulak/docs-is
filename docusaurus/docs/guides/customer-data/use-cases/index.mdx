<h1>Customer data service use cases</h1>

These guides show common patterns for integrating with the Customer Data Service from your application. They capture visitor activity even before an account is created, and unify it into a single profile that follows the user across sessions, devices, and applications.

---

<Prerequisites>

## Before you begin

- [Enable Customer Data Service](../enable-customer-data-service.mdx) for your organization.
- A [machine-to-machine (M2M) application](../../applications/register-machine-to-machine-app.mdx) is registered for your backend to call the Customer Data Service APIs, granted the `internal_cds_profile_create`, `internal_cds_profile_update`, and `internal_cds_profile_view` scopes and configured to issue tokens with the `iam-cds` audience.
- The [profile attributes](../guides/manage-profile-attributes.mdx) that your activity maps to are defined, each with the merge strategy it should use.
- The [unification rules](../guides/manage-unification-rules.mdx) that decide when two profiles are the same person are defined.

---

</Prerequisites>

## Use cases

| Read | Use this when |
|---|---|
| [Unify anonymous and registered profiles](self-registration.mdx) | A visitor interacts anonymously, then signs up, and their activity is consolidated into the new profile. |
| [Accumulate data across multiple devices](cross-device.mdx) | A user interacts anonymously across multiple devices and then signs in, and their activity is consolidated into a single profile. |
| [Stitch user profiles across websites](lead-stitching.mdx) | A visitor interacts anonymously across multiple applications or domains, and their activity is consolidated into a single profile based on a shared identifier. |
